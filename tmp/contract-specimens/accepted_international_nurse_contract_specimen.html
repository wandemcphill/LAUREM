COMMENCEMENT, STATUS AND CONTINUOUS EMPLOYMENT

Employment begins on the commencement date stated above, subject to the employee satisfying all lawful pre-employment conditions, including identity, right to work, safeguarding, occupational health, references and any role-specific checks. Continuous service is calculated from the recognised employment commencement date.

APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES

The employee is appointed as Registered Nurse. The employee will perform nursing duties appropriate to the role, competence, training, experience and professional registration status, and will comply with reasonable instructions, care plans, policies, safeguarding requirements and client assignment arrangements. No duty will require the employee to work outside their lawful scope of practice or competence.

INTERNATIONAL RECRUITMENT AND IMMIGRATION STATUS

The employee is being recruited internationally. The intended immigration route is Health and Care Worker visa. The sponsored occupation is 2237 (Registered Nurse). Laurem will issue sponsorship documentation only where it is legally authorised to do so and where all sponsorship requirements are met. Immigration permission is a legal matter for the employee and the relevant UK authorities. The employee must maintain lawful immigration status and promptly provide evidence of any change affecting the right to work.

PROFESSIONAL REGISTRATION AND NMC

The employee confirms that their professional registration journey is: CBT passed; OSCE scheduled; working towards full NMC PIN registration. The employee must complete all applicable NMC requirements and maintain registration necessary to perform the role. Where the employee starts work before full NMC registration under a lawful supervised/practice arrangement, duties, supervision and pay will be limited to the lawful conditions of that arrangement. Full registration is expected Within 8 months of UK arrival in accordance with NMC guidelines. Failure to obtain or maintain the required registration may result in changes to duties, suspension, or termination where lawful and after applicable procedures are followed.

The normal contractual hours are 37.5 hours per week, ordinarily rostered across days, nights, weekends and public holidays according to operational requirements and applicable rest and working-time protections.
