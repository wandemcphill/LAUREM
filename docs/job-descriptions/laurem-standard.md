## 1. Job purpose

The postholder contributes to the safe, respectful and professional delivery of services on behalf of Laurem Caregroup Ltd. Duties must be performed in accordance with the employment contract, job description, applicable procedures, training, competence and lawful instructions.

---

## 2. Principal responsibilities

1. Carry out the duties associated with the role safely, professionally and within authorised responsibilities.
2. Treat service users, colleagues, families, visitors and partner organisations with dignity and respect.
3. Follow applicable safeguarding, confidentiality, health and safety and infection-control requirements.
4. Maintain accurate records and communicate important information promptly.
5. Report incidents, concerns, complaints and risks through the approved Laurem procedures.
6. Complete required induction, mandatory training, supervision and competency requirements.
7. Maintain appropriate professional boundaries and protect confidential information.
8. Use approved Laurem workforce systems for rota, attendance, timesheets, leave and communication where instructed.

## 3. Person specification

### Essential
- Lawful right to work in the UK and completion of applicable pre-employment checks.
- Professional and respectful communication.
- Reliability and commitment to safe working practices.
- Ability and willingness to work within training, competence and authorised duties.
- Commitment to confidentiality, equality and safeguarding.

### Desirable
- Previous relevant experience.
- Relevant role-specific qualifications or training.
- Experience using electronic records or workforce systems.

---

## 4. Working within competence

The employee must only perform duties for which they are appropriately trained, competent, authorised and, where required, supervised.

---

## 5. Attendance, development and flexibility

The employee must attend scheduled duties punctually, follow applicable rota, absence and timesheet procedures, participate in supervision and training, and cooperate with reasonable lawful changes to duties within the contractual and competence framework.
